### AI drug discovery and cheminformatics

- **CHEESE** — I invented CHEESE and built its core vector retrieval system for molecular shape and electrostatic similarity. [Product](https://cheese-new.deepmedchem.com/) · [Paper and supplement](https://github.com/Deep-MedChem/cheese-paper)
- **SynthonOR** — Reaction-grounded molecular retrieval using synthon fingerprints. [Code](https://github.com/mireklzicar/synthonor) · [Demo](https://synthonor.mireklzicar.com/)
- **SynthonGPT** — A synthon-conditioned transformer for retrieval in makeable chemical spaces. [Demo](https://synthongpt.mireklzicar.com/) · [Technical report](https://synthongpt.mireklzicar.com/report.pdf)
- **SynFactor** — A research prototype that distills molecular property models into reusable synthon factors for fast approximate scoring. [Code, experiments and limitations](https://github.com/mireklzicar/synfactor)
- **SynthonMap** — Cartography of combinatorial chemical spaces using factorized representations and density maps. [Public computational core](https://github.com/mireklzicar/synthonmap) · [Interactive viewer](https://synthonmap.mireklzicar.com/) · [Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=qUoHw1xkIM)
- **OPDiv** — Selecting high-scoring compounds under explicit diversity constraints, with optimization bounds and certificates. [Code](https://github.com/mireklzicar/opdiv) · [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28665)
- **SynthonBench** — Benchmarks for budgeted optimization in reaction-defined chemical spaces. **Work in progress:** extending the benchmark and evaluating search methods with live oracles. Code is not currently public.

I’m also exploring diversity-controlled retrieval with SynthonGPT. New experiments and proposed interfaces remain work in progress; the links above identify the available artifacts.
